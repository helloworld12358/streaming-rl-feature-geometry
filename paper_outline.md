# Mini-paper outline

1. Introduction: predictive representations for streaming RL under partial observability.
2. Research question: whether imposed feature geometry improves online linear control.
3. Environment: continuing partially observable T-maze with delayed cue echo.
4. Methods: fixed mixed GVF bank, causal transforms, SARSA(lambda), baselines.
5. Metrics: control, prediction, hidden-state information, representation geometry, update stability.
6. Results: smoke/validation and full remote tables/figures.
7. Negative results and limitations: representation metrics may improve without reward; Gaussian matching can harm binary-cue structure.
8. Reproducibility: commit SHA, configs, commands, artifacts.
